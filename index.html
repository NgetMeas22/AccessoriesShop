Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about delivery, warranties, switch compatibility, and return policies.

How fast is shipping within Cambodia and internationally?
Orders placed before 2:00 PM are dispatched the same day! Deliveries in Phnom Penh and Kampot typically arrive within 24 hours. For other provinces, delivery takes 1 to 2 business days via trusted couriers (Vireak Buntham, J&T). Free express shipping applies automatically to all orders over $50.
What is covered under the 2-Year Official Warranty?
All electronic accessories (mechanical keyboards, wireless mice, audio headsets, USB-C docks, and 4K webcams) come with our 2-Year Chey Hardware Guarantee. This covers factory sensor defects, wireless connectivity failures, and switch PCB malfunctions. We replace defective units directly without waiting for overseas repairs.
Are your mechanical keyboards hot-swappable?
Yes! All our mechanical keyboards, including the Apex Pro 75 and Keychron K3 Pro, feature 5-pin universal Kailh hot-swap sockets. This allows you to pull out switches and pop in any 3-pin or 5-pin Cherry MX style switch (Gateron, Kailh, Outemu, Akko) without any soldering iron required.
What payment methods do you accept at checkout?
We support instant ABA PAY / KHQR mobile scans, Visa, MasterCard, and Cash on Delivery (COD) across Cambodia so you can inspect your package safely before payment.
How do I claim a return or refund?
If you are not 100% satisfied with your item within 30 days of receiving it, simply contact us via Telegram (+855 96 666 666) or email with your order ID. As long as the product is in its original box with included cables, we issue an immediate exchange or full refund.
Can I customize the aviator cables or mousepads?
Absolutely! We offer custom coil lengths, aviator connector color finishes (matte black, chrome silver, rose gold), and custom graphic printing on our AeroDesk XXL mousepads for corporate workspaces and esports teams. Reach out via our Contact form for bespoke specs.
